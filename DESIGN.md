# Design system — Testimonials

## Overview

Testimonials are treated as a darkroom contact sheet: quotes are proofs in a tray, status is an exposure state, and the add form is a developer tray rather than a generic admin card.

## Colors

- #1B1511 — darkroom brown-black.
- #F5E8CC — photographic paper and readable light surface.
- #F5A25D — safelight orange and action.
- #3A2418 — featured proof ground.
- #BCA889 — pencil/secondary copy.

## Typography

Georgia carries the human, quotable voice. Courier New is used for lab labels, frame numbers, and local-state notes.

## Layout

A darkroom header and oversized editorial statement lead into a light contact sheet. Proofs form an intentional contact grid; the final developer tray returns to the dark room and gives the user a focused entry path.

## Elevation & Depth

The paper contact sheet is the primary change of plane. Proofs are dark inset frames with a thin inner border, creating photographic depth without generic card shadows.

## Shapes

Frames are rectangular and crisp. Borders behave like film and paper registration marks; no rounded card stack is used.

## Components

- Safelight header
- Exposure readout
- Searchable contact sheet
- Quote proof with Featured/Review/Archive state
- Develop-a-proof form

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep authors and exact quote text visible.
- Do let state affect the proof treatment without inventing performance claims.
- Don't claim a quote is verified, published, or from a real client unless supplied.
- Don't turn testimonials into star ratings or a fake analytics dashboard.
